Privacy Policy

Charmr ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information through our mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information (if you log in): email address
  • Usage Data: device type, operating system, app usage metrics
  • GPT Interactions: summaries and message data (used to improve context, personalization, train our own AI models, and for commercial purposes)
  • AI Prompts: When you use the app, your prompt and message context may be shared with third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI) to generate responses. These prompts may include conversational history and any information you provide. We may use this data for training our own AI models and for commercial purposes, including selling to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App
  • To deliver personalized content and GPT suggestions
  • To manage subscriptions and billing
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To train and improve our own AI models and algorithms
  • To develop new features and enhance existing functionality
  • For commercial purposes, including selling data to third parties
  • To create and sell derivative products and services

3. Data Sharing

  • We may sell, license, or otherwise share user data with third parties for various purposes, including but not limited to:
    • AI model training and development
    • Market research and analysis
    • Product development and improvement
    • Commercial applications and services
    • Direct marketing and advertising
    This includes both anonymized and non-anonymized data.
  • We may share data with service providers (e.g. OpenAI, Firebase, RevenueCat) strictly to operate the App.
  • Third-party AI providers process the data only to deliver the requested functionality and do not store or reuse it for training unless their own terms allow and you opt-in (e.g. OpenAI's data usage policy).
  • We may use user data to train and improve our own AI models and for commercial purposes, including selling to third parties. While we may anonymize some data, we are not obligated to do so.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and best practices to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your data by contacting privacy@example.invalid
  • You can manage consent and app permissions via your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Charmr is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

8. Contact Us
